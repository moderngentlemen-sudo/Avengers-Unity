Assemble your team

Press any key, or any button on a gamepad, to join. Up to four players: the keyboard plus three pads. Pick a hero, then an Assist Callout partner nobody is playing.

Keyboard: A / D to browse, K to confirm, L to go back. Pad: d-pad or stick, A, B.

Controls

Every button does something. Keyboard on the left of each row, gamepad on the right. G fills your Hero Gauge so you can try Supers and Ultimates.
